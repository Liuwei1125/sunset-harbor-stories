## Harbor modifications / 港湾修改

The model has been modified: normalized to metres and a 4.720 m overall length;
materials adjusted; connected mesh components grouped under wheel and steering
pivots; screen UVs replaced; original Harbor front/rear racing displays added;
and a reduced geometry version exported. The high-detail body is based on the
credited artist mesh, not authored from factory CAD.

车漆、轮胎、玻璃、金属与内饰材质经过调整；轮组、方向盘和显示面可独立驱动。
屏幕为本项目自行绘制的赛车 UI，不是特斯拉车机界面截图。预览里的车速、功率与圈速演示由合成输入驱动，不构成实车性能数据。
